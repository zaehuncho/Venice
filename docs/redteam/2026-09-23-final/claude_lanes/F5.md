### CL3-F5-002 LOW — Between-session reorder: the saved stable ID is not used to re-resolve the index (residual CL2-P3-003)

- **Components:**
  - `RemotePlaySession.cpp:2523-2529` passes the saved `captureCardIndex` plus `ORION_CAPTURE_SELECTED_ID`.

  - `StreamSetupForm.qml` `ccPicker.currentIndex`.

- **Failure path:**

  2. The ID now lives at index 1, so index 0 fails `_explicit_device_matches`.

  5. The notice reads "Venice is reading a camera that isn't your capture card (Integrated Webcam)…", although nothing is being read.

### CL3-F5-003 LOW — The "duplicated" qualifier trips on a healthy card during game content and tells the customer to change hardware
- **Evidence tier:** observed in the owner's live log (dev launcher `D2A9920F`, same sidecar source generation). Fixture reproduction.
- **Component:** `capture_card_backend.qualify_capture_feed` (`_QUALIFY_DUP_BAND` 25–70 % with unique fps below 51); `remote_play_orchestrator.py:7060-7140`.
- **Preconditions:** a healthy 60 fps card, with game frames that repeat (a menu, replay or transition rendered below 60 unique fps).
- **Failure path:**
  - `logs/orion_native.log:36890-36962` (session of 2026-09-23 12:00 local):
    - `Capture feed NOT qualified for timing: duplicated (dup_pct=40 unique_fps=36)` at 17:01:27Z.
    - Notice: "Your capture device is repeating frames (only about 36 real fps of the 60 Venice needs), so the bot will not shoot. Set it to 1080p60 or 720p60, or use a USB 3.0 port."
    - "steady at 60 fps again" 3 s later.
    - Repeated at 17:02:25Z.
  - The same windows show `preview_stats fps=60.0 gap_ms mean=16.7`, so the card itself was delivering a clean 60.
- **Impact:** two false hardware alarms within about 2 minutes of a healthy session, plus brief benches. This drives support contacts and customers changing ports and settings for nothing. It fails closed (no silent fire).
- **Reproduction:** `qualify_capture_feed(60.0, 18.6, 40.0, 36.0, 60, samples=120)` returns `(False, 'duplicated', 'dup_pct=40 unique_fps=36')`, exit 0.
- **Fix:** attribute duplicates to the card only when raw frame gaps also show the pattern (paired identical buffers at the source cadence), or when the condition persists over N windows during gameplay (meter or court seen). Otherwise treat it as content and stay silent. At minimum, drop the hardware advice from a transient notice.
- **Closure test:** replay the 09-23 12:00 capture segment (or a synthetic 30-fps-content-in-60-fps-carrier fixture) and expect no `duplicated` notice. A real 30-padded-to-60 card fixture must still trip it.

### CL3-F5-004 LOW — The production sidecar still honours `%USERPROFILE%\Desktop\NexusVision\settings.json`, so owner-PC package tests are not fresh-machine tests
- **Evidence tier:** source-confirmed. Presence observed: `C:\Users\aaron\Desktop\NexusVision\settings.json` exists, mtime 09-23 12:33. The modules are compiled into rc1 (strings present in `OrionSidecar.exe`).

- **Closure test:** a unit test with `__compiled__` set and a planted Desktop settings.json asserts defaults, or the explicit native config, are used. Then run the rc1 package on a profile without the Desktop folder (VM) and compare the detector-config log lines against the owner PC.

### CL3-F5-006 MEDIUM — 30 Hz is a customer choice that self-qualifies for timing, with no validation (M-33 "untested 30 Hz")
- **Evidence tier:** fixture reproduction plus source-confirmed.
- **Components:**
  - `StreamSetupForm.qml:336-357` offers the model `["30 Hz", "60 Hz (recommended)"]`.
  - `AppConfig.h:100-113` snaps to {30, 60, 120}.
  - `RemotePlaySession.cpp:2533-2537` sets `ORION_CAPTURE_FPS`.
  - `qualify_capture_feed` computes `min_fps = requested × 0.85`.
- **Preconditions:** the customer selects 30 Hz (for example with a cheap 1080p30 dongle, or on the belief that it fixes stutter).
- **Failure path:** the requested rate becomes the yardstick, so a 30 fps feed qualifies and the bot fires on 33 ms frames. The whole timing stack (lead, extrapolation, onset feedforward, green-window aim) was fitted and graded at 60 fps. The low-fps notice itself says "Venice needs a steady 60".
- **Impact:** a supported-looking mode with unmeasured, likely worse, early/late variance. Customers will blame the bot. This is not a silent fire on a wrong source, but it is fire authority on an unvalidated route.
- **Reproduction:** `qualify_capture_feed(30.0, 34.0, 0.0, 30.0, 30, samples=60)` returns `(True, '', 'raw_fps=30.0 gap_p95_ms=34.0')`. The same feed with requested 60 returns `(False, 'low_fps', 'raw_fps=30.0 < 51.0')`. Exit 0.
- **Fix:** qualify against the validated floor (60) regardless of the requested rate, or keep 30 Hz as preview-only with an explicit "bot needs 60 Hz" notice. Alternatively, validate 30 Hz with a graded session and scoreboard before offering it.
- **Closure test:** with a 30 Hz request and a 30 fps feed, expect `low_fps` or `preview_only`, and no fire authority. The picker copy should match.

### CL3-F5-007 MEDIUM — An explicit capture pick is overridden by "card-name" adoption (CL2-P3-007 is still open in rc1); the name hint is broad
- **Evidence tier:** fixture reproduction. Device names are a hypothesis for the specific products named.
- **Components:**
  - `capture_card_backend.py:1186-1222` (`_candidate_indices` appends every card-named index after the explicit one) and `:1245-1263` (`route_basis='card_name'`).
  - `identity_verified()` at `:864-892`, where `card_name` never consults `ORION_CAPTURE_SELECTED_ID`.
  - `remote_play_orchestrator.py:4446-4495` (the guard **adopts** a `card_name` index and rewrites `ORION_CAPTURE_CARD_INDEX`).
  - Scope at `:823-827` (`classes[index]=='card'` bypasses the selected-ID check).
- **Preconditions:** the customer explicitly picked device A. A is busy (OBS) or asleep. Another device's name contains a card hint.
- **Failure path:**
  1. The walk tries A (busy), then opens B.
  2. `route_basis=card_name` and `identity_verified()` is True.
  3. The route guard adopts B for cold timing.
  4. Only fps qualification and the detector now stand between B and fire authority.
- **Name hint breadth:** `_CARD_NAME_HINTS` includes `"capture"` and `"avermedia"`. `_classify_name` returns **card** for `screen-capture-recorder` (a common DirectShow desktop-capture filter) and for `AVerMedia Live Streamer CAM 313` (an AVerMedia webcam).
- **Impact:** the customer's explicit choice is not authoritative. On a two-card rig, or a PC with a virtual "capture" filter, timing can run on a device the customer did not pick. A wrong-device fire still needs meter-shaped pixels on B, so this is authority drift, not an observed wrong fire. It is also the bug that makes A4-001's "inventory-gated" statement only partly true.
- **Reproduction:** `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B C:\Users\aaron\AppData\Local\Temp\claude-F5\f5_identity.py`. Names `USB Video|AVerMedia Live Streamer CAM 313`, selected ID = index 0, and index 0 `_open` returns busy. Output, exit 0:
  ```
  classify 'screen-capture-recorder' card
  candidates [0, 1]
  start True opened [0, 1] resolved 1 basis card_name identity_verified True
  ```
- **Fix:** CL2-P3-007's own required fix.
  - When `ORION_CAPTURE_SELECTED_ID` is set, adoption is allowed only for the index whose moniker ID equals it (enumeration drift is then resolved by ID). Any other device is `uncertain`, with a "your selected card is busy/asleep" notice.
  - Keep the name fallback only for an **unselected** fresh install, and require exactly one card-named device there.
  - Narrow `"capture"` (whole-word or vendor list) and add `screen-capture`, `live streamer cam`, `pw3` and similar to the webcam/virtual deny list.
- **Closure test:** the same fixture yields `start False`, failure `busy`, and no index 1 opened. A drift fixture (same ID moved to index 1) still adopts with `configured`.

### CL3-F5-008 MEDIUM — The device inventory is frozen for the sidecar's whole lifetime; an in-process reopen after unplug/replug trusts stale identity
- **Evidence tier:** fixture reproduction plus source-confirmed. This widens the accepted AUD-A4-001 residual.
- **Components:**
  - `ORION_VIDEO_DEVICE_NAMES/IDS` are inserted once at sidecar launch (`RemotePlaySession.cpp:2540-2544`).
  - There is no device-change handling anywhere in native (no `WM_DEVICECHANGE` / `RegisterDeviceNotification`).
  - `capture_card_backend._device_names/_device_ids` read `os.environ` on every reopen.
  - The orchestrator's ~2 s card retry and self-heal reopen create a new backend with the same index.
- **Preconditions:** mid-session, the card disconnects (USB power, cable, hub) and DirectShow re-enumerates, for example with a webcam now at the card's old index. It can also reconnect in a different order.
- **Failure path:**
  1. The reopen at the configured index opens whatever is there now.
  2. `route_basis=configured`, because the stale ID at that index still equals the selected ID, so `identity_verified()` is True.
  3. The route guard sees DSHOW at the expected index, so the route "matches".
  4. Only the fps/contract qualification and the detector remain.
- **Relation to the rc1 sheet:** the sheet's "not attested across a same-second hot-plug" understates this. The window is the whole session (minutes to hours), not a single enumerate→open race.
- **Impact:**
  - A webcam can be opened and granted capture identity (LED on, privacy concern).
  - The notice shows the card as fine.
  - Timing authority rests on fps plus the detector alone.
  - A persisted warm posterior keyed on the stale `device-id=` could be restored or updated against a different device (hypothesis; not traced to the persistence write).
- **Reproduction:** `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B C:\Users\aaron\AppData\Local\Temp\claude-F5\f5_stale_inventory.py`. Launch inventory `USB Video|Integrated Webcam` with USB Video selected. Mid-session only the webcam remains, now at index 0. Output, exit 0:
  ```
  reopen True index 0 physical Integrated Webcam basis configured identity_verified True env_name_at_index USB Video
  ```
- **Fix:** any capture reopen after the first successful open must use a **fresh** inventory. Either:
  - (a) the sidecar enumerates DirectShow monikers itself (COM through `comtypes`/`pygrabber`, or a tiny native helper) immediately before each open and matches the selected ID; or
  - (b) native listens for `WM_DEVICECHANGE`/`CM_Register_Notification` for video-input interfaces, revokes capture authority, and restarts the sidecar with a new inventory.

  The long-term fix remains a moniker-bound open (`IMoniker::BindToObject`), so the opened handle is the attested device.
- **Closure test:**
  - The stale-inventory fixture must return `identity_verified` False (or `start` False with `absent`).
  - A hardware drill on the owner rig (unplug the HD60 X mid-session with a webcam attached, then replug in a different port) must show authority revoked, the card re-identified by ID, and no webcam open.
