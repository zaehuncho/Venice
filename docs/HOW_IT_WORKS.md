# How Orion Works

*Plain-English explanation for customers and support. No jargon, no hype — this is honestly what
the product does.*

## The short version

Orion **watches your shot meter on the screen and times your release to the green** — the same
thing your eyes and thumb are trying to do, just faster and more consistent.

It's pure computer vision plus a controller press. Nothing is touched in the game.

## What actually happens when you shoot

1. **You hold the shoot button** like normal. Orion doesn't decide when you shoot — you do.
2. **Orion watches the screen.** Your PS5's video feed comes into the PC (through an HDMI capture
   card, or through the Remote Play stream itself). Orion looks at the same shot meter you see
   and tracks the fill rising, frame by frame.
3. **It times the release.** Orion measures how fast the meter is filling, predicts the moment it
   will reach the green window, and accounts for the small delay between the screen and the
   console.
4. **It releases the button for you** — one ordinary controller input, sent through PlayStation
   Remote Play, exactly as if you'd lifted your thumb at the perfect moment.

That's the entire product: **see the meter, release on green.**

## What Orion does NOT do

- It does **not** read or modify the game, its memory, or its files.
- It does **not** install anything on your PS5 or jailbreak anything.
- It does **not** change your stats, your player, or anything inside 2K.
- It does **not** press anything a human couldn't press. The only thing the console ever
  receives is a normal controller input over Sony's own Remote Play.

From the game's point of view, Orion is indistinguishable from a player with very good timing.

## What it needs from you

- **A video feed**: an HDMI capture card is the best setup (cleanest, fastest picture). Orion can
  also work from the Remote Play stream without a card.
- **A decent connection**: wired Ethernet for the PC and PS5 is strongly recommended. Orion can
  only be as fast and steady as the video feed you give it.
- **A few warm-up shots**: Orion calibrates itself to your setup — your jump shot, your
  connection, your delay — and gets sharper over the first shots of a session.

## Honest expectations

Orion dramatically improves consistency, but no tool can promise 100%. A laggy Wi-Fi spike, a
heavily contested shot, or an unusual animation can still cost a shot. Green rates depend on
your connection quality and setup — that's also why we recommend the trial and a wired
connection first.

If something isn't working (no meter detected, black screen, no releases), it's almost always a
setup issue — capture card, HDCP, or Remote Play pairing — and support can walk you through it
in minutes.
