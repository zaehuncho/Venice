### THE FUNDAMENTAL WALL + COMPLETE BYPASS (first-principles, 2026-07-09)
**The true physical wall to 100%:** NBA 2K samples input on its 60Hz sim tick (±16.7ms), and its **phase is UNOBSERVABLE** — we infer timing from lagged rendered frames, not from when the game samples input. So there's an irreducible ±16.7ms uncertainty in which tick catches the release. **True 100% on a sub-tick (single-frame-perfect) target is physically impossible** over an async networked pipeline — can't reliably hit a target narrower than an unseen tick. BUT the wall only bites if the target < wall. Two pure-software bypasses:
- **A — make the window > the wall (the plateau):** the cap-hold holds at 100% for ~50ms = 3× the 16.7ms tick. If releasing anywhere in the plateau scores GREEN (the H6 live-confirm hinge), the tick-quant+jitter fit INSIDE the window → ~100% green reachable. Don't beat the wall — make it irrelevant.
- **B — observe the wall's phase:** **120fps CAPTURE is available** (PS5 120Hz frame-doubled output + Elgato 1080p120, even though 2K content is 60fps) → oversamples the 60Hz tick → localizes each update transition + the sim-tick phase to **~8.3ms** (partial phase recovery, shrinks the wall ~2× — NOT more fill samples). Place the release nearer a known tick. Even at 60fps, the **discrete green-flash/swish is a sim-tick-locked event** → a clean phase reference the analog meter can't give; track it over shots to recover phase from the aliasing beat.
- **BIG — tick phase is NOT a hard ~8.3ms floor; it's a STABLE PERIODIC parameter → lock it with a PLL to SUB-MS.** The 60Hz tick is crystal-stable (period ~16.667ms, phase drifts slowly). A phase-locked loop over tick-locked events integrates a stable clock → phase σ shrinks ~1/√N below the single-sample resolution. Once locked, ALL future tick times are known → schedule the release for the intended tick → the ±16.7ms quant DISSOLVES. **PLL reference = the METER'S OWN step transitions** (the fill advances one discrete step per sim tick; at 120fps capture each step is localized to ~8.3ms). These are tick-locked BY DEFINITION, happen DURING the rise (real-time, not post-hoc), and there are dozens/shot → PLL locks to sub-ms with NO extra sensor (the reference IS the meter we already read). **AUDIO REJECTED (user domain knowledge):** shot/swish/rim sounds fire AFTER the meter is timed and 2K audio is buffered/decoupled — useless as a reference. **This MOVES the wall:** with phase locked, the binding term becomes DELIVERY jitter (can the release ARRIVE within ±half-tick of the locked tick? = the transport/frame-quant work — sub-ms wired, fat-tail Wi-Fi is the risk). Caveats: PLL locks on the step rate; step-jitter from capture PTS is clean (done); delivery jitter < half-tick. **Aggressive bypass of the tick wall — all software.** (Far-fetched, flagged: rolling-shutter scanline sub-frame timing — likely noise-dominated.)
- **NET:** literal 100% on the tightest window = physically impossible; **~100% reachable if BOTH plateau-scores-green AND tick-phase-recovered.** Both software, no hardware.
