## 2. "What Orion is / isn't" honesty blurb (pin in `#faq`)

> ## What Orion is — and what it isn't
>
> **What Orion is:**
> - **Computer vision + a controller press.** It reads the shot meter off the video feed on your PC and fires your release at the green window. That's the whole product.
> - **A calibration tool.** It learns *your* build's jumper over the first 10–15 shots and re-tunes when 2K changes the meter.
> - **An assistant and trainer.** It helps you hit timing windows reflexes make hard, and watching the overlay teaches you where green lives.
> - **Honest about its ceiling.** It improves your consistency every possession. It runs only as fast as your Remote Play feed.
>
> **What Orion isn't:**
> - **It never touches the game or your console.** No game files, no memory, no injection — nothing runs on your PlayStation. Your console plays a completely stock game, exactly as Sony and 2K shipped it.
> - **It's not a guarantee of a green on every shot.** Latency spikes, stream quality, and in-game factors are real, and no software fixes physics.
> - **It's not a fixed-timer macro.** It reads the actual meter, frame by frame, and calibrates to you.
> - **It's not magic on a bad setup.** Congested Wi-Fi, HDCP left on, or the controller paired to the console will hold it back — that's why we say so before you buy.
>
> Questions about account safety, setup, or whether it'll work on your rig? Ask in **#faq** or take the **free 72-hour trial** and see for yourself.
